Founder · Chiropractor

Dr. Mike Valier, DC

Dr. Mike Valier, DC, is the founder and chiropractor at Center for Better Balance, bringing over two decades of experience delivering highly personalized, results-driven care.

Raised in Manitou, Colorado, Dr. Mike developed a deep connection to movement, the outdoors, and an active lifestyle early on, an influence that continues to shape both his life and his clinical philosophy.

More than 20 years ago, he founded Family Chiropractic with a vision of providing thoughtful, individualized care. That vision has since evolved into Center for Better Balance, where the focus is on whole-body wellness and long-term results.

Dr. Mike believes that true health is built through a balance of mobility, flexibility, strength, and proper function, and every treatment plan is tailored to the unique needs and goals of the patient. His approach integrates hands-on chiropractic care with advanced techniques to restore movement, enhance performance, and support lasting wellness.

He works with patients across all stages of life, including mothers throughout pregnancy and postpartum, infants, children, athletes, and individuals in every season of life, adapting care to meet each individual where they are.

Outside the clinic, Dr. Mike lives the lifestyle he promotes. He has a passion for travel and adventure, spending time mountain biking, climbing, playing hockey, and exploring the outdoors. Above all, he values time with his fiancée and son, who are at the center of everything he does.

Philosophy

Dr. Mike's four-part balance

01

Mobility

Joints that move freely through their full range.

02

Flexibility

Muscle and tissue with room to lengthen.

03

Strength

Enough capacity to hold good movement under load.

04

Function

Your body using all three in daily life and sport.

Physical therapy · Athletic training

Kevin Feldman, DPT, ATC

Kevin is a Certified Athletic Trainer with over a decade of experience in athletic training, personal training, and rehabilitation.

He is known for his multifaceted approach to movement therapy, combining advanced techniques like dry needling, therapeutic exercise, and evidence-based rehabilitation strategies.

Kevin's career spans a wide range of settings, including sports teams, rehabilitation clinics, and personal training facilities. He has developed expertise in orthopedic assessment, injury prevention, and personalized exercise programming, working with diverse populations ranging from young athletes to active adults. His commitment to patient education ensures that clients not only recover from injuries, but also gain a clear understanding of how to maintain their health long-term.

With a focus on empowering clients through education and movement, Kevin helps individuals achieve their health and fitness goals, whether they are recovering from injuries or striving for peak performance.

Office manager

Chantelle Ibanez

Born and raised in Colorado Springs, Chantelle brings over 20 years of customer service and management experience to the Center for Better Balance.

As our dedicated office manager, she's the friendly face that keeps everything running smoothly, from scheduling appointments to making sure each patient feels welcomed and cared for from the moment they walk through the door. Chantelle has a true passion for helping others and takes pride in making sure every patient feels seen, supported, and at ease.

Her journey with the Center for Better Balance began long before she joined the team: she was a patient of Dr. Mike's for many years, which gives her a deep understanding of the care and compassion that defines the practice.

When she's not at the front desk, you'll find her in the mountains tearing up the trails on her 4-wheeler, cruising the streets on her Can-Am Ryker, or spending time with her three beloved pit bulls. A fan of fast cars and Colorado's outdoor lifestyle, she brings energy and enthusiasm into everything she does.
