Center for Better Balance · Design System v0.1

Built for
motion.

A small, quiet system drawn from the clinic itself: slate walls, walnut floors, navy leather and warm lamplight.

Inside

Tokens · Type · Components · Scroll motion. Everything here lives in assets/css and assets/js/motion.js.

Premium, never busy.

Three rules every page should pass. When something feels loud, it usually breaks one of these.

01

The room is the brand

Real photography of the clinic carries the color and warmth. The interface stays neutral so the place can speak.

02

One gesture per section

Each section gets one motion idea: an image opening, a line rising, a track sliding. Never several at once.

03

Calm type, clear action

A regular-weight serif for feeling, a precise sans for facts, a small mono for wayfinding. Booking is always one tap away.

Color

Sampled from the room.

Every color is pulled from the clinic's interior photos. Plaster for light, slate and spruce for dark chapters, walnut and leather for depth, and ochre as the one small signal.

Motion

Slow in, settled out.

Smooth scrolling (Lenis) plus GSAP ScrollTrigger, driven by data attributes so pages stay plain HTML. Motion eases out and settles, never bounces. With reduced motion turned on, everything simply appears.

data-split

Headlines rise line by line from behind a mask.

data-reveal · data-reveal-group

Blocks fade and rise 28px with an expo ease-out. Grouped items stagger 80ms apart. Used on almost everything.

data-clip · the image opens as you scroll

data-parallax · the image drifts inside its frame
