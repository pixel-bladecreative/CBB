Featured · GyroStim

Help for dizziness and balance.

GyroStim is advanced multi-axis neuro-rehabilitation. You sit in the chair while your clinician rotates it to train the systems that keep you steady. We use it to target dizziness, recalibrate balance and support concussion recovery.

  • DizzinessVertigo and unsteadiness
  • BalanceCoordination and reaction
  • ConcussionRecovery support
